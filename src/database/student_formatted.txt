# Student Dataset Attributes

This dataset contains attributes for both **student-mat.csv** (Math course) and **student-por.csv** (Portuguese language course).

---

### Demographic Information
1. school – Student's school
   - "GP" = Gabriel Pereira
   - "MS" = Mousinho da Silveira

2. sex – Student's gender
   - "F" = Female
   - "M" = Male

3. age – Student's age (15 to 22)

4. address – Home address type
   - "U" = Urban
   - "R" = Rural

5. famsize – Family size
   - "LE3" = Less or equal to 3
   - "GT3" = Greater than 3

6. Pstatus – Parent's cohabitation status
   - "T" = Living together
   - "A" = Apart

---

### Parental Information
7. Medu – Mother's education (0–4)
   - 0 = None
   - 1 = Primary (4th grade)
   - 2 = 5th–9th grade
   - 3 = Secondary education
   - 4 = Higher education

8. Fedu – Father’s education (0–4) (same scale as Medu)

9. Mjob – Mother’s job
   - "teacher", "health", "services", "at_home", "other"

10. Fjob – Father’s job
   - "teacher", "health", "services", "at_home", "other"

11. reason – Reason for choosing school
   - "home", "reputation", "course", "other"

12. guardian – Student’s guardian
   - "mother", "father", "other"

---

### Study & School Life
13. traveltime – Home → School travel time
   - 1 = <15 min
   - 2 = 15–30 min
   - 3 = 30–60 min
   - 4 = >1 hour

14. studytime – Weekly study time
   - 1 = <2 hours
   - 2 = 2–5 hours
   - 3 = 5–10 hours
   - 4 = >10 hours

15. failures – Number of past class failures
   - 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4 (≥4 failures)

16. schoolsup – Extra educational support (yes/no)
17. famsup – Family educational support (yes/no)
18. paid – Extra paid classes in subject (yes/no)
19. activities – Extra-curricular activities (yes/no)
20. nursery – Attended nursery school (yes/no)
21. higher – Aspires to higher education (yes/no)
22. internet – Internet access at home (yes/no)
23. romantic – In a romantic relationship (yes/no)

---

### Social & Health Factors
24. famrel – Family relationship quality (1–5)
25. freetime – Free time after school (1–5)
26. goout – Going out with friends (1–5)
27. Dalc – Workday alcohol consumption (1–5)
28. Walc – Weekend alcohol consumption (1–5)
29. health – Current health status (1–5)
30. absences – Number of school absences (0–93)

---

### Grades
31. G1 – First period grade (0–20)
32. G2 – Second period grade (0–20)
33. G3 – Final grade (0–20, target variable)

---

### Note
- Some students (≈382) appear in both datasets.
- They can be identified by matching identical attributes (see annexed R file).
